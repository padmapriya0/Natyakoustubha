about The Dance School

Natya Koustubha, we are a team of passionate individuals.

Bharatanatyam: The word "Bharatanatyam" is derived from the words "Bhavam" (expression), "ragam" (music), "talam" (rhythm), and "natyam" (dance).

Kathak: Kathak is a classical Indian dance form characterized by intricate footwork, expressive gestures, and storytelling through rhythmic movements.

Kuchipudi: Kuchipudi is a classical Indian dance form from Andhra Pradesh, known for its expressive storytelling, intricate footwork, and graceful movements.

Odissi: Odissi is a classical Indian dance form originating from Odisha, known for its graceful movements, intricate footwork, and expressive poses, often depicting mythological stories.

Kathakali: Kathakali is a classical Indian dance-drama from Kerala, known for its elaborate costumes, makeup, and storytelling through expressive gestures and movements.

Mohiniyattam: Mohiniyattam is a classical Indian dance form from Kerala, characterized by graceful, fluid movements, subtle expressions, and a focus on the feminine aspect of storytelling.
